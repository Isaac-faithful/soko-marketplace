HOW IT WORKS

Simple on both sides.

01 — DISCOVER

Choose what feels like home.

Browse authentic products from approved merchants across Nigeria, Ghana and Kenya.

02 — PAY LOCALLY

Pay the exact local price.

See dispatch, carrier updates and delivery status in one place, with support when something goes wrong.
